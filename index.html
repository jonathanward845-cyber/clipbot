ClipBot

Stream highlights, cut into Shorts.

What it does

ClipBot is a private tool that takes popular public clips from live streams, edits them into short vertical "Top 5" compilations with captions, and uploads them to YouTube channels owned and run by the ClipBot operator.

How it uses YouTube

ClipBot uses the YouTube Data API only to upload finished videos (with title, description and tags) to the operator's own channels, after the operator approves each video. It does not read, change or delete anything else on a YouTube account, and it is not offered to the public.

Credit to creators

Every video credits the original streamer and links to their channel and the source clips. Creators who would like their content removed can get in touch using the contact details on the privacy policy page.
