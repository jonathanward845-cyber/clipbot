Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information ClipBot ("the app") accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected. ClipBot is a private tool used only by its operator to upload videos to YouTube channels the operator owns.

Information the app accesses

When a YouTube channel is connected, the app asks Google for two permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload to upload videos to that channel;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly, used only to read the connected channel's name once, so the operator can confirm the right channel was linked.

Google then gives the app an access token and refresh token for that channel.

The app does not access your email address, contacts, viewing history, comments, subscribers, analytics or any other Google account data.

How the information is used

  • Tokens are used only to upload videos, with their title, description and tags, to the connected channel.
  • Uploads happen only after the operator approves each video.
  • No data is used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

Storage and sharing

  • Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No Google user data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • The app has no user accounts, analytics, cookies or tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ClipBot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ClipBot uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove ClipBot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is removed, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the operator's computer. You can also ask for deletion by contacting us (see below), and we will do it within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
